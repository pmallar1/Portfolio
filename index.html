About

Computer science background, designer's instincts.

Patrick Mallari is a full-stack web developer based in Los Angeles, with a solid computer science foundation and a passion for the web across both front-end and back-end technologies.

He earned a Bachelor's degree in Graphic Information Technology, focused on full-stack web development, from Arizona State University. Since then his work has spanned e-commerce platforms to content management systems, always aiming for seamless experiences and robust functionality.

Outside of code, Patrick hikes, builds games and plays them, and pulls inspiration from all of it into what he ships.
