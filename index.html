About

Technical problem-solver. Creative thinker.

I'm Patrick Mallari, an IT Administrator based in Southern California. My work centers on supporting the technology people depend on every day, from troubleshooting user issues and deploying computers to maintaining access, connectivity, and reliable business systems.

I support IT operations across multiple locations and work with technologies including Active Directory, Group Policy, SonicWall, VPNs, VoIP, and remote support tools. I also create documentation and improve processes to make recurring tasks more consistent and manageable.

My background extends beyond IT. I earned a bachelor's degree in Graphic Information Technology, focused on Full Stack Web Development, from Arizona State University. My experience in web development, graphic design, and photography gives me another way to approach problems: consider the technical requirements while keeping the user experience in mind.

I'm interested in continuing to grow in IT, taking on more responsibility, and combining technical and creative skills to improve how people work with technology.
